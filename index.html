Always on

The part that matters

Never invent a contact. Never hide a decision.

An agent that will embellish your history is not working for you, it is spending your reputation for a maybe. This is a hard constraint, not a setting.

What Jobby does

  • Reads your resume and tells you what it does not say, instead of filling the gaps in.
  • Writes every change to your dossier to an audit log with a timestamp and who made it.
  • Tells you when it did not send something, and why.
  • Paces itself: a daily cap and a one-click kill switch.
  • Asks before it throws away a role, because a rejection is data, not a verdict.

What Jobby will not do

  • Claim an employer, title, date, degree or number that is not in your dossier.
  • Invent a reference, a contact, or an availability date.
  • Round up your results. 42% stays 42%.
  • Apply you to a role you are not qualified for.
  • Tell you a job is secured before there is a record of it.

Questions

Straight answers

Will it just send applications without asking me?

Yes, up to a daily cap you control, and only to roles your dossier supports. Switch on draft mode and it will prepare everything and wait. The kill switch stops all outbound sending immediately.

Who sees my resume?

It is parsed locally on your machine, then the dossier is stored on your own relay. Nothing is sent to a third-party service to be read.

What happens after I get a job?

The mission shifts from finding income to not losing it: comp review, notice period, and the 90-day plan. The consulting and contract tracks stay live as a second income stream.

Can it edit my profile on its own?

No. It only writes to your dossier when you tell it something or confirm a correction, and every single change is recorded with who made it and whether you confirmed it.

Do I need a Google account?

No. It is optional. Connecting one lets Jobby send from your own address and read replies without you pasting them in, and connect your Drive.

01 — Jobby McJobberson Live

Your personal job agent

Upload a resume and, within a minute, you have a full dossier, an honest read on which income tracks your background supports, and a plan ordered by what pays you soonest. Then Jobby sources roles, researches the target, writes the outreach, sends it, follows up, and negotiates — until income is secured, and after.

  • Four search tracks, opened from your own resume
  • Sends from your own address; replies reach your inbox
  • Every dossier edit is logged with who made it
  • Daily send cap and a one-click kill switch

02 — GrayTech Security Live

Know who is in the room

Face detection and recognition over a monitored scene. People cross a scan line, each one is turned into a 512-number vector and matched against enrolled references by cosine similarity — arriving as a named identity with a confidence score, or UNKNOWN if nobody clears the bar. The threshold is not a guess: every score is logged and the operating point is derived from where errors actually occur in your own traffic.

  • Detection and recognition networks; the model is fixed, not retrained
  • Self-calibrating threshold — false accepts and false rejects measured, not assumed
  • Correcting an identification enriches that profile immediately
  • Cannot enrol a stranger: adaptation only ever touches people already known

How we build

Three rules we do not trade away

Autonomous software is only worth trusting if it cannot quietly damage the person who switched it on. These are enforced in code, not in a policy document.

01 Never invent

An agent may not claim an employer, a title, a date, a number or a credential that is not in the record. It states what it could not confirm instead of filling the gap.

02 Log everything

Every change to a person's profile, and every outbound message, is written to an append-only audit trail. You can always answer what it did and when, without trusting anyone's memory.

03 Stay stoppable

Autonomy is bounded by a cap you set, and a kill switch that takes effect on the next send. Unrecoverable actions are not on the menu.
